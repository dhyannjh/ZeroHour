# ZERO HOUR

**A high-risk, high-reward mental challenge game.**

Created for **Kruu × GDAI Game Jam 2026: ZERO HOUR**.

Infiltrate a secure digital network, solve challenges under pressure, and push your luck to maximise your haul. Every challenge brings greater rewards — but the longer you stay, the higher your chances of getting caught.

## Gameplay

- **Solve Challenges:** Test your mental math, pattern recognition, logic, memory, and attention through timed challenges.
- **Earn Credits:** Clear challenges to score points and build up your unbanked haul.
- **Manage Exposure:** Every step deeper into the network increases the risk of detection.
- **Know When to Extract:** Secure your credits by extracting before the system catches you.
- **Face Boss Challenges:** Take on special challenges that push your abilities further.
- **Use Tactical Perks:** Gain additional ways to manage risk and extend your run.

## The Risk-Reward Loop

**Infiltrate → Solve → Earn → Risk It or Extract**

Every decision matters. Do you play it safe and bank your earnings, or push deeper for a bigger reward?

Be careful: **getting detected ends your run, and all unbanked credits are lost.**

## How to Play

Detailed instructions, controls, and gameplay rules are available directly in the game's UI. Simply download the ZeroHour.exe file and run it.

## About the Project

ZERO HOUR was created collaboratively as part of the **Kruu × GDAI Game Jam 2026**, built around the theme of high-stakes decision-making and mental challenges under pressure.

Our goal was to turn math and logic puzzles into a tense gameplay experience where success depends not only on solving challenges, but also on managing risk.
